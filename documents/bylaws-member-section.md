Section 4.1 Members
The Members of this Church shall:
• Consist of those who have complied with the requirements set forth in Section 4.2 of this
Article.
• Have properly presented themselves for membership in accordance with the procedures
determined by the Elder Board.
• Have been enrolled as Members on the membership roster.
• Not hold more than one membership category per person.

Membership in this Church shall not vest in any Member any distributions from the Church
during the existence of the Church but shall only entitle the Member to vote at meetings of the
Members. Membership shall not be assignable inter vivos by any Member, nor shall
membership vest to any personal representative, heir or devisee.
Section 4.2 Requirements for Membership
A Member must acknowledge that he/she has accepted Jesus Christ as his/her personal Lord
and Savior, give assurance of a desire to live a consistent Christian life, submit to the authority
of Scripture and accept the Church Doctrinal Statement. A Member will also agree to support
the Church By-Laws, UPCC Core Values, financially support, and serve in the ministry of the
Candidates for membership shall be 18 years of age or older. A Member will have received the
ordinance of water baptism by immersion following conversion and a willingness to receive the
Lord's Supper according to scriptural teaching. Candidates must have attended and completed
membership classes, and must be interviewed by an Elder or designee of the Elder Board.
Those candidates who meet with the approval of the Elders or designees of the Elder Board
present and voting (at the meeting where candidates are considered) shall be approved for
membership.
Section 4.3 Types of Membership
There shall be two types of membership: Voting Members and Non-Voting Members.
Individuals who elect to be Non-Voting Members shall agree to comply with all of the By-Laws
set forth governing Voting Members with the exception being that Non-Voting Members do not
have the following privileges:
• Voting rights
• Hold any Ministry Leadership positions(s)
Section 4.4 Removal of Members
Membership of any individual shall cease by the acknowledgment of the Elder Board and upon
the happening of any of the following events:
A . Death
A deceased Member's name shall be removed from the Church membership roll.
ResignationA Member may voluntarily request in writing to be removed from the Church
membership roll, provided that the Member is not currently the subject of disciplinary
proceedings. A Member's name shall be removed from the Church membership roll
when a Member joins another church.
The Elder Board shall periodically review the Church membership roll. Any Member
who, without reasonable cause, has neglected to attend services of the Church for a

Section 4.5 Discipline of Members
Members who become unfaithful to the Lord Jesus and His Word, or live inconsistently with a
Christian profession, or refuse to abide within the framework of the Church's Doctrinal
Statement and By-Laws, may be excluded from membership by Simple Majority vote of the
Elder Board after procedurally following the steps outlined in Matthew 18:15-20 and I
Corinthians 5.
If the individual's disobedience or divisiveness persists, the Elder Board shall remove him/her
from the membership list following a Simple Majority vote of the Elders. Such a one may be
restored following a change of heart and a trial period, upon the Board of Elder's discretion.
Section 4.6 Place of Meetings
Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in these Bylaws any meeting whether regular, special
or adjourned of the Members of this Church may be held at any place which has been
designated by the Elder Board.
Section 4.7 Regular Annual Meetings
The membership of the Church shall meet at least once each calendar year. This meeting shall
take place at least one calendar month prior to the end of the fiscal year.
The Elder Board shall make a public announcement from the pulpit at least two Sundays in
advance of the meeting and in writing (regular mail and/or electronic mail) to the membership
before the meeting. Notice of the annual meeting shall specify the place, the day and the hour
of the meeting, and the general nature of the business to be transacted. A quorum is necessary
if any decisions are to be made at the annual meeting. A quorum is defined by Section 4.9 of
the By-Laws.
The Lead Elder (or his designee) shall prepare the agenda and shall conduct the meeting.
Section 4.8 Special Meetings
Special meetings of the Church membership may be called only by the Elder Board.
The Elder Board shall make a public announcement from the pulpit at least two Sundays in
advance of the meeting and in writing to the membership before the meeting.
Notice of any special meeting shall specify the place, the day and the hour of the meeting, and
the general nature of the business to be transacted.

Section 4.9 Quorum
At all meetings, whether regular or special, a quorum shall be 50% or more of the Voting
Members who are eligible to vote. At the discretion of the Elder Board, the quorum may include
both members physically present at the meeting or members voting by absentee ballot.
If a quorum is not present at the time of the vote, an alternative date will be scheduled at the
earliest date possible.
Section 4.10 Absentee Voting
The privilege of absentee voting will be extended at the discretion of the Elder Board upon
individual request.
Section 4.11 Powers Reserved to Voting Members
In addition to any powers of the Members specifically set forth in these Bylaws or reserved to
the Members by the Corporations Code of Pennsylvania, the following powers are reserved to
the Voting Members as noted below. A quorum must be present for a Church meeting to be
official. Voting Members are allowed only one vote per member.
A. Business shall require a 3/4th majority vote of the quorum.
1) Business requiring a Majority 3/4" vote shall include:
Election / Hiring / Removal of the Lead Elder.
Amending, changing or otherwise altering the Doctrinal Statement.
Acquisition or sale of Church real estate.

Amending, changing or otherwise altering these By-Laws.
vi. Election of Lay Elders and Deacons.
vii. Budget
2) Business requiring the 3/4" Majority will be done by secret ballots.
3) Tabulation of secret ballots (yeas and nays, not abstains) will be performed by at
least two Voting Members.
4) Business requiring a 3/4" majority must receive a positive vote from at least 75%
of the quorum.
5) Secret ballots shall be destroyed right after the ballots have been counted.
The By-Laws, including the doctrinal statement, may be altered or amended at any
duly called meeting of the church by a ¼ Majority vote. In order for such an
amendment to be considered the proposed amendment or alteration must be
published to the congregation at least 30 days prior to any action being taken. Further,
the Church shall supply proper notice of the meeting, consistent with the requirement
