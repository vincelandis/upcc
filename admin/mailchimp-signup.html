Why Connect?

One of our core values is Relational Living. We desire biblical relationships to be cutlivated in all of our connection ministries. Growing together in Christ unites the young and old in age and both the new and mature believer. Our Lord was passionate about developing authentic relationships as He daily poured into His disciples while on earth. Our prayer is that you find UPCC a place where you connect with other believers as we pursue a deeper knowledge and passion for Jesus Christ together.
